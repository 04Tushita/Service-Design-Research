Research Methodology & Ideation Notes

Documenting the research journey across the Double Diamond framework: from generative field interviews to co-design brainstorming sessions.

Double Diamond Stages

Iterative divergence and convergence across four distinct phases.

Stage 01 • Discover

Exploratory Inquiry

4 in-depth semi-structured interviews and 3 competitive ecosystem benchmarks uncovering frontline operational barriers.

Stage 02 • Define

Synthesis & Framing

Affinity diagramming, thematic clustering, archetype definition, and formulation of core Point of View (POV) declarations.

Stage 03 • Develop

Ideation & Mapping

Crazy 8s ideation, participatory sketching, end-to-end service journey mapping, and low-fidelity user flow wireframing.

Stage 04 • Deliver

Concept Validation

Walkthrough evaluations of Flow A vs. Flow B with target stakeholders, synthesizing feedback for the final recommendation.
